Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who this applies to

JarvisOS is a private, self-hosted personal assistant operated by a single individual for their own use. It has no other users and does not accept sign-ups.

What data is accessed

When the operator connects their own Google Account, JarvisOS may read that account's Google Calendar entries and Gmail messages using Google OAuth, strictly in read-only mode.

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer the operator's own questions about their own schedule and messages. It is never sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

Where the data is stored

The application runs on hardware owned by the operator. OAuth tokens and any cached data are stored encrypted on that device. Queries may be sent to a large language model provider for processing.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens. Local data can be deleted by the operator at any time.

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on the associated GitHub repository.
